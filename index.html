Why groups lose money they never see

The cost of the gap

Five systems, five truths

A restaurant group does not run on one system. It runs on a till, a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp group, an accountant's file, and four delivery tablets. Each holds a different version of the truth.

Every seam between two systems is a place where money goes missing.

  • The till knows the sale — but not what the plate cost.
  • The spreadsheet knows stock — until someone forgets to update it.
  • Every aggregator tablet needs its own 86'ing.
  • The accountant knows last month — and only what was re-typed.

One system, end to end

MiM is not a POS with reports bolted on. It is one database that every department writes into and reads from — so there is one version of every number, and it is today's.

  • Money enters once, at the till, and is never re-typed.
  • The P&L and the cockpit run the same arithmetic.
  • Every riyal out is matched line-for-line with the bank.
  • Marketing sees demand in portions — never a riyal.

Unknown is never zero

Every row is judged or says why not — here 12 of 12. A shelf with no price on file shows its shortage as real and its money as unknown, never rounded to zero to make the report look complete.

Blind is not variance

Recipe coverage sits beside the gap — here 100%, no sold line without a recipe. A dish sold without one is quarantined, not counted as a saving — a dish nobody costed is a hole in the map, not good news.

Grams never become pieces

“20g of saffron” against a kilo shelf used to read as 20kg — a 1000× lie in the food cost and a shelf that empties on paper. Conversions are same-dimension only, or they refuse.

Nothing is seeded

No dish is given a made-up cost at 30% of its price to fill the table. Uncosted means uncosted, and it says so on the screen until someone enters the recipe.

Straight answers

Questions we get asked

What happens when the internet goes down?
The till keeps selling. Orders, payments and voids queue locally and settle with the engine when the line returns. The one rule that matters: an empty answer is never treated as truth — if the till cannot reach the engine it shows the last known floor, menu and branch list rather than a blank screen that looks like a closed restaurant.
Does it handle Qatar labour law and WPS properly?
Yes, and not as a report you export somewhere else. The payroll file is the real SIF format the bank accepts. End-of-service benefit, rest-day pay, the Article 70 cap and the wage floors under Law 17/2020 are computed by the system, and it flags a salary that sits below the legal minimum before payday rather than after.
Can I get my data out?
Every table exports to a spreadsheet, and the underlying journal is append-only — so your history is complete rather than a snapshot of today. If you ever leave, you leave with the whole record. We would rather earn the renewal than hold your numbers hostage.
Do I have to enter every recipe before it is useful?
No. The till, the floor, payroll, the store and the P&L all work from day one. Food cost is the part that needs recipes — and until a dish has one, the system says "not costed yet" rather than inventing a number. An unknown is never shown as a zero, and it is never quietly averaged into your margin.
What about Arabic?
Every screen runs right-to-left in Arabic, including the receipt. It is not a translation layer bolted on top — the layout itself flips, so nothing ends up reading backwards or colliding.
Does it connect to Talabat, Snoonu and the others?
Not yet, and we will not tell you otherwise. The delivery bridge is built and running dormant; the Talabat partner application is in progress. Everything currently connected is listed under Integrations as "Running today", and everything that is not is listed as "Coming". We would rather lose a sale than be found out on the install day.
Who actually uses this?
We do. MiM runs a 17-branch, six-brand group in Doha, and it was built because we were losing money in the gaps between five systems. We are customer number one, and we have no third-party case studies to show you yet — when we do, they will be real and named.
What hardware do I need?
A Mac or Windows machine for the till, and any tablet with a browser for waiters, the chef display and the hostess desk — tablets pair by scanning a QR code and stay paired to that one branch. Standard 80mm kitchen printers. If you already have tills, we will look at whether they can be reused before you buy anything.

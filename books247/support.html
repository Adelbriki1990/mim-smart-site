Common questions

I bought coins or VIP but don't see them. Open the app, go to Me ▸ Restore Purchases, and make sure you're signed in to the same account.

How do I cancel VIP? VIP renews weekly through Apple. Cancel anytime in iPhone Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before it renews.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Me ▸ Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and data.

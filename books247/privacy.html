24/7 Books — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

24/7 Books ("the app") is published by MiM. This page explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details — if you create an account: your name and email address, or the identifier Sign in with Apple gives us (Apple can hide your real email). You can use the app as a guest without an account.
  • Reading and wallet data — books you save or like, your reading progress, coins, streaks and unlocked chapters, so they stay in sync across your devices.
  • Purchases — handled entirely by Apple. We receive Apple's signed receipt to deliver your coins or VIP; we never see your card or Apple Pay details.
  • App activity — simple events such as "chapter unlocked" or "purchase completed", to improve the app.

Advertising

Free readers see ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads only if you allow tracking when iPhone asks. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads are shown, and you can change your choice anytime in the app under Me ▸ Privacy & ad choices. VIP members see no ads. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Where data is stored

Account and reading data are stored with our cloud provider, Supabase. We do not sell your personal data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app reminds you about free chapters and daily coins. You can turn them off in iPhone Settings.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and its data at any time in the app: Me ▸ Delete Account.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Some stories are for readers aged 16 and over and are marked with a content note.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@mim-smart.com
